# CTC Phase 1: design and content direction

## Reviewed materials
- Club presentation: https://view.genially.com/697f74e9bb78a059e35f09d3 (reviewed October 5, 2026).
- Current website: https://www.chicagotrainingclub.com/.
- All 31 supplied JPEGs in ../Pictures, including the beach and lakeshore promotional posters.
- Supplied official horizontal SVG wordmark; official favicon from the current site.

The presentation is authoritative for origins, mission, membership and training formats. It mixes Season 005 and 006 labels and contains template placeholder metrics; do not publish those labels or placeholder numbers as facts. The 150+ sessions figure is a historical presentation claim, not a live counter. No testimonials or current event dates are supplied.

## Essential journeys
1. Home: understand Chicago calisthenics and CTC's welcoming club culture; explore training or join.
2. /the-club: origins, values and the mission behind CTC.
3. /chicago-calisthenics: open sessions, handstand clinics and research & development; first-session guidance.
4. /community: real local photography, browsable gallery and official social links.
5. /join: free membership, practical questions, official Instagram for current session details and direct questions.

## Visual direction
An independent Chicago sports journal: warm ivory, near-black, neutral accents, condensed oversized headlines and generous editorial whitespace. The supplied monochrome one-arm handstand leads the homepage. The official wordmark has sole branding prominence in the navigation. A sticky club film expands from an inset frame to viewport width as visitors scroll, reversing as they scroll back. A moving photographic collage, oversized typography, dark community section and staggered real-product collection add varied rhythms. Native scrolling is preserved. Reduced-motion preferences disable scroll choreography and automatic playback; data-saving preferences also prevent autoplay. The film is muted, with a visible pause/play control, and pauses when offscreen.

## Content constraints

The hero positioning “Chicago’s largest calisthenics community” was requested directly by the club owner on October 5, 2026. It is owner-provided marketing copy, not a ranking independently established by the presentation. The main headline is “Chicago. Raise the bar.” The training and FAQ accordions use shared natural-height open/close transitions, with reduced-motion support and inert collapsed content.
Free membership does not imply every session is free: the presentation describes free and low-cost sessions. Posters establish all skill levels, water, chalk/bands and past lakefront locations, but do not establish a current calendar. The live site's official Instagram is the verified contact destination. Its email text is incomplete; no email address is invented. No booking form pretends to submit, and no ecommerce functionality ships in Phase 1.

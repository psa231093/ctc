## Essential journeys
1. Home: understand Chicago calisthenics and CTC's welcoming club culture; explore training or join.
2. /the-club: origins, values and the mission behind CTC.
3. /chicago-calisthenics: open sessions, handstand clinics and research & development; first-session guidance.
4. /community: real local photography, browsable gallery and official social links.
5. /join: free membership, practical questions, official Instagram for current session details and direct questions.

## Visual direction
An independent Chicago sports journal: warm ivory, near-black, a sunset-inspired Pink Line accent, condensed oversized headlines and generous editorial whitespace. Original photographs carry the identity. Chicago six-point stars punctuate the layout. Numbered captions, split layouts, inset imagery and a photographic contact-sheet rhythm avoid repetitive feature-card sections. Motion uses short section reveals, restrained image transitions and optional native page transitions; reduced-motion preferences disable movement. No scroll hijacking or autoplay media.

## Content constraints
Free membership does not imply every session is free: the presentation describes free and low-cost sessions. Posters establish all skill levels, water, chalk/bands and past lakefront locations, but do not establish a current calendar. The live site's official Instagram is the verified contact destination. Its email text is incomplete; no email address is invented. No booking form pretends to submit, and no ecommerce functionality ships in Phase 1.

## Deferred verification
Confirm current session dates, pricing (if any), locations, accessibility details, coach biographies, contact email and approved image credits before adding these. Confirm that selected photographs have web publication permission. Public domain cutover and Search Console verification are launch tasks, not implied by a private review deployment.

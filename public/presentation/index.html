08 / The digital brand · Wordmark

ONE MARK.
FULL CONFIDENCE.

The official horizontal wordmark is the signature. Everything around it gives it room to work.

One cap-height of clear space
x

Proposed clear-space rule for ongoing digital use.

01 / Integrity

Preserve the original

Use the SVG. Keep its proportions. Never redraw, stretch, rotate or apply effects.

02 / Legibility

Give it a quiet surface

Black on light. Light on dark. Avoid placing it across faces, bars or busy detail.

03 / Hierarchy

Let one signature lead

The Chicago star is an accent. It never replaces the name or competes with the wordmark.

11 / The digital brand · Layout & motion

ENERGY.
WITH INTENTION.

Motion should feel like progress. Layout should make the next move obvious.

A motion study / the CTC assembly motif

BUILDTHE

Deliberate. Reversible. Never in the way.

01 / Give content space

Alternate dark and light sections. Keep text to readable widths. Let one headline lead each composition.

02 / Move with purpose

Use transform and opacity. Keep loops out of reading areas. Slow down expressive movement on mobile.

03 / Keep everyone included

Respect reduced motion. Keep focus visible. Use at least 44px touch targets and preserve text contrast.

Sources & project notes

This presentation documents CTC’s delivered website and proposed digital brand guidelines, reviewed October 6, 2026.

Club and legacy website

CTC’s source presentation and the previous website. Broken destinations and incomplete contact links were directly reviewed. The mobile-navigation issue was reported by the team.

Performance

Train with us: October 6, 2026, 2:18 PM CDT. Homepage: October 6, 2026, 2:47 PM CDT. These are lab tests, with no field data. Scores vary with test conditions. No old-site performance comparison is claimed.

Technology and optimization

React 19.2.6, Next.js 16.3.4 and TypeScript 5.9.3, verified in the source repository. The 53% image reduction compares 72 matching assets: 6,469,554 WebP bytes and 3,059,637 AVIF bytes. It is an asset-library comparison, not the reduction of every page’s payload.

Brand and scope

All photography and the official wordmark were supplied by CTC. Colors and fonts reflect the delivered website. Logo clear-space and voice guidance are proposed for ongoing digital use. Phase 1 links to Shopify; an integrated store remains Phase 2.
